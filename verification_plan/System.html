risc_lsu_03Byte-enable generation per access type and address (from rtl cv32e40p_load_store_unit.sv):
SW: addr[1:0]=00->4'b1111; misaligned first beat 01->1110, 10->1100, 11->1000; second beat 01->0001, 10->0011, 11->0111.
SH: 00->0011, 01->0110, 10->1100, 11 (crossing)->first 1000 then 0001.
SB: 00->0001, 01->0010, 10->0100, 11->1000.
Only these patterns may ever appear on data_be_o.		lsu_passive_agent

risc_lsu_04Misaligned handling: the LSU never raises address-misaligned exceptions. A word load/store at addr[1:0]!=00, or a halfword at addr[1:0]==11, is performed as TWO bus transactions; the transfer for the lowest address is issued first, followed by (aligned base+4) with the complementary byte-enables; the memory transfers for the two parts are merged by the core into one load/store view. All other offsets need a single transaction.Verified in rtl cv32e40p_load_store_unit.sv (data_misaligned_o, misaligned_st) and the databook LSU chapter.lsu_passive_agent, uvm_scoreboard

risc_lsu_06Load data alignment and extension: LB/LH sign-extend the addressed byte/halfword; LBU/LHU zero-extend; LW returns the full word; for misaligned accesses the two response words are assembled (first-response low bytes kept in rdata_q, combined with the second response) according to the address offset.uvm_scoreboard
